# Surface brief — index (one-page portfolio)

## Scope and visitor mode

Single page, whole site. Mode: **Experience** — the work (14+ years of infrastructure, in terrain form) leads from the first viewport; the interface recedes.

## Audience, job, proof

Recruiters, peers, referrals. Job: size Stavros up in under a minute, then connect. Proof: real roles and dates 2010→today, real credentials (MSc Kingston, MTCNA, Cisco, ECPE), real projects. No invented claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The network is the terrain. Crete's topography, drawn as live circuit infrastructure, carries the whole page — the category default (dark hero, icon cards, neon glow) is refused.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep ink-navy ground; desaturated blue-teal contour bands as the page's surfaces; one electric cyan accent reserved for real signals (links, focus, data highlights). Archivo semi-expanded black caps for display, Archivo for body, Martian Mono only for true data (dates, durations, coordinates). Contour linework, survey marks, and mono readouts are the component language.

**STORY:** The visitor lands on living terrain that reacts to cursor and scroll, reads the name and role set in that terrain, scrolls down through a timeline of real infrastructure work, and exits via one unmistakable action: connect on LinkedIn.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-viewport generative contour field (canvas, animated, pointer-reactive, parallax on scroll). Top-left: nav. Center-left mass: "STAVROS ANTONIOU" in expanded caps at display scale; under it the full verbatim headline; under that, "Heraklion, Crete, Greece" with a mono coordinate readout (35.3387° N / 25.1442° E) as real data. One quiet scroll cue. No CTA button in the hero — the contact lives at the journey's end.

**FORM:** Dark-technical topographic survey, code-led (no image generation available), signature interaction = the living contour field with scroll parallax and pointer drift, reveal choreography varied per section (clip-path wipe for the timeline, staged type for About, none for data lists).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
